How a submission is checked

Deadline is start plus duration

An exam that starts at 10:00 AM and runs for 60 minutes closes at 11:00 AM.

The deadline minute counts

A paper submitted exactly at the deadline is on time. Anything after it is late.

Delay is in whole minutes

A late paper reports how many full minutes passed after the deadline.

Impossible times are rejected

The duration has to be more than 0, and a paper can't be submitted before the exam starts.
